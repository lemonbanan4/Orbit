# Orbit — Video Ad Production Kit (YouTube / Google App Campaigns)

Durable home for the ad scripts and production workflow, so they never live
only in a chat again. Google App campaigns pull video from YouTube links
(upload as **unlisted** is fine), 15–30s, ideally in three ratios:
16:9 (landscape), 9:16 (vertical), 1:1 (square). Up to 20 videos per campaign.

## The one rule that overrides everything

This ads account was suspended for "Unacceptable business practices" and only
just won its appeal. Every frame must be literally true:

- No invented user counts, ratings, or testimonials.
- No fake "real person" endorsements. Cosmica is safe **because** she is
  openly the app's own fictional AI-coach character — never present an AI
  avatar as a real human customer.
- Only show real app UI. No mocked-up screens with features that don't exist.
- Pricing claims: "Free to download" is true. "7-day free trial on the annual
  plan, then paid" is true. Nothing else about price.

## Recommended workflow (HeyGen + CapCut hybrid)

1. **HeyGen** → generate ONLY the Cosmica voiceover (and optionally a short
   3–4s avatar bookend at the start or end). Do not assemble the full video
   in HeyGen — its media layout tools are weak for app-demo footage.
2. **Screen recordings** → capture real app footage on a phone/simulator
   (dashboard with a live streak, completing a habit + XP pop, journey map,
   Cosmica chat, ambient/sanctuary screen). Recordings beat static
   screenshots: motion holds attention and Google favors it.
3. **CapCut** → assemble: hook text → app footage cuts every 2–3s → VO on
   top → captions ON (most YouTube ad views are muted) → end card.
4. Export all three ratios from the same CapCut project, upload to YouTube
   unlisted, add the links as video assets in both Google Ads campaigns.
