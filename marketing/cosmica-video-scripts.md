# Orbit — Cosmica Video Scripts (TikTok / YouTube / Reels)

**Purpose:** Short vertical (9:16) app-promo videos for the Orbit Google Ads
App campaigns (US + Sweden) and organic TikTok.
**Character:** "Cosmica" — the calm, cosmic guide voice of Orbit (the app's AI
coach persona). Warm, grounded, a little dreamy. NOT a hard-sell announcer.
**Length target:** 15–25 seconds each.
**CTA every time:** "Download Orbit."

> Note on honesty: these are legit product claims. Do NOT reintroduce fake
> discounts or invented stats — that's what nearly got the ads account (and
> possibly the app) flagged. Keep every claim true.
