# Orbit — 5 Native TikTok Concepts (phone-shootable)

**Goal:** native, non-ad content that gets views in the habit / self-improvement
niche, with Orbit woven in as the *setting*, not the sales pitch. Post from a
real phone, one real account, 1–3x/day. CTA ("link in bio") goes in the BIO,
not the caption. One trending sound per post. Shoot vertical 9:16, good light.

> Honesty rule (same as the video scripts): real claims only. No fake
> discounts, no invented stats. The app is genuinely gamified with streaks, XP,
> an AI coach, ambient audio, and a cosmic journey — lean on what's true.

---

## 1. "The Restart" — relatable POV (post this FIRST, it's your strongest)
- **Format:** screen recording of opening Orbit → your streak / journey, with big
  text overlay.
- **Hook (on-screen text, first 1s):** "restarted my habits 14 times this year 💀"
- **Then (text switch ~2s in):** "the 15th time actually stuck 🌌" as the app's
  streak / XP / journey shows on screen.
- **Caption:** the 15th time was the charm 😭
- **Hashtags:** #habittracker #adhd #productivity #routine #selfimprovement
- **Sound:** a trending "sad → hopeful" or glow-up transition sound.
- **Length:** 8–12s.

## 2. "Oddly Satisfying" — screen recording, no face
- **Format:** close-up screen record of checking off habits — XP filling, streak
  ticking up, confetti/animation.
- **Hook (on-screen text):** "POV: checking off habits hits different when it
  looks like this ✨"
- **Caption:** the little dopamine hit is unmatched 🪐
- **Hashtags:** #satisfying #dopamine #productivity #aesthetic #app
- **Sound:** calm aesthetic / ASMR-ish trending audio.
- **Length:** 7–10s.

## 3. "Storytime" — talking head (builds trust + reach)
- **Format:** you, front camera, casual and natural (NOT reading a script), with
  a few seconds of app b-roll cut in.
- **Hook (say it + caption it):** "I couldn't stick to a single habit for 3
  years. here's what finally worked."
- **Body:** kept failing → tried making it a game → streaks + an AI coach that
  learns your patterns made you actually show up → now on an X-day streak.
- **Caption:** not sponsored, I just genuinely use this now 🌌
- **Hashtags:** #selfimprovement #habits #storytime #glowup
- **Sound:** your own voice (original audio — talking-heads perform on original).
- **Length:** 20–35s.

## 4. "3 Tricks" — value / listicle
- **Format:** talking head or text-over-b-roll, quick cuts, "3 things."
- **Hook:** "3 habit tricks that actually stuck (after years of failing)"
  1. make it a game — streaks + XP so showing up is rewarding
  2. shrink the first step until it's stupidly easy
  3. track it somewhere you'll actually see it
  (show Orbit as the tool for #1 and #3)
- **Caption:** #2 quietly changed everything for me
- **Hashtags:** #productivity #habits #studytok #discipline
- **Sound:** upbeat trending or original voice.
- **Length:** 20–30s.

## 5. "Apps that feel like a game" — recommendation montage
- **Format:** screen-record montage — the cosmic journey, milestones unlocking,
  ambient audio screen, the theme.
- **Hook (on-screen text):** "apps that make being productive feel like a video
  game 🎮"
- **Caption:** the space theme is genuinely unreal 🌌
- **Hashtags:** #appsyouneed #productivity #gamified #fyp
- **Sound:** trending "recommendation"/list sound or chill electronic.
- **Length:** 10–15s.

## CapCut — how to build each one (mobile, ~10 min each)

### One-time setup (do this before all of them)
1. **Screen-record Orbit on your phone** (iOS: Control Center → Record; Android:
   Quick Settings → Screen record). Capture 20–40s of: opening the app, your
   streak, checking off a habit (the XP/confetti animation), the cosmic journey,
   milestones. This raw footage feeds every video.
2. Open **CapCut → New Project** → import the screen recording.
3. Tap the canvas ratio → set **9:16** (vertical, full screen).

### Adding the hook text (the most important 1 second)
1. **Text → Add text** → type the hook. Keep it BIG, white, bold, top-third of
   the screen (thumbs cover the bottom).
2. Drag the text clip on the timeline so it appears at **0:00** and holds ~2s.
3. For a text switch (e.g. concept 1), add a **second** text clip starting where
   the first ends.

### Adding a trending sound
1. In CapCut: **Audio → TikTok** (or add it later inside TikTok itself — often
   easier). If adding in TikTok: export CapCut *without* music, then pick the
   trending sound in the TikTok editor at upload.
2. Trim the sound so the "drop"/beat lands on your hook or the satisfying moment.

### Captions (accessibility + watch-time)
1. **Text → Auto captions** → CapCut transcribes your talking (concepts 3 & 4).
2. Position captions in the **middle**, not the very bottom.

### Export for TikTok
- **1080p, 30fps** (or higher). Tap **Export** → save to camera roll → upload to
  TikTok from your phone. (Add sound in TikTok if you skipped it in CapCut.)

### Per-concept build notes
- **#1 Restart:** screen-rec of streak/journey + two text clips (14x → 15th).
  Trending sad→hopeful sound. No voice needed.
- **#2 Satisfying:** tightest crop on the check-off + XP/confetti moment. Slow it
  slightly (CapCut → speed 0.8x) for the satisfying feel. Calm sound.
- **#3 Storytime:** film your face first (front cam), then cut 3–4s of app b-roll
  over the middle. Auto-captions on. Original audio (your voice).
- **#4 3 Tricks:** talking head OR text-over-b-roll. Add three numbered text
  clips (1, 2, 3) timed to each point. Upbeat sound.
- **#5 Game montage:** 4–5 quick cuts of the journey/milestones/theme, ~1.5s
  each (CapCut: split clips, delete the boring bits). Trending list sound.

## Posting notes
- **1–3 posts/day, spaced hours apart.** Never hourly (spam signal).
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — engagement velocity drives reach.
- **Reuse:** re-film the same concept with different hooks/covers/sounds; each is
  a fresh at-bat for the algo.
- **US reach:** English content on a real account spills over once it performs;
  post a slot at ~7–10pm US Eastern (≈1–4am CET) to catch US prime time.
- **Timeline:** expect ~2–4 weeks of consistent posting before something catches.
  Normal. Consistency compounds.
